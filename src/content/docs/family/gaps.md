The MUSTs each app still misses, then the SHOULDs open in several apps. Termina's and Mirada's rows were read again
on 2026-11-09, and every app's command ids then too; the rest is from the [feature matrix](../matrix/)'s survey,
with the MUSTs a review of edition 5.0.0 found open and missing here added that day, each read in the app's code at
the commit that edition pins, as were the gaps the decisions on that review opened. A command id, catalog key, chord,
flag, environment variable, property or repository file a rule's statement names is required as spelled; a class,
style or branch name there is Editora's, and an app's equivalent meets the rule
([Coding agents](../../coding-agents/#what-a-rule-looks-like)).

## Missing MUSTs

Six MUSTs are open across the family. The rows leave OS-02, WIN-11, WIN-12 and PAUTA-18 out, and say for MENU-16
and CLI-12 what each app lacks. No app has the one `Os` class that alone reads
`os.name` ([OS-02](../../same-on-every-os/#os-02)): 51 files read it in Editora, 20 in Termina, 19 in Torrenta, 15 in
Mirada, 4 in nux and 3 in Modula, and eight more of Editora's tell Windows apart by `File.separatorChar` or
`File.pathSeparatorChar` instead. No app fixes the order of a dialog's buttons
([WIN-11](../../window-shell/#win-11)): none calls `setButtonOrder`, so Editora, Torrenta, Termina, Mirada and nux
draw theirs in JavaFX's order for the OS, and Modula's one dialog has a single button. No app has a UI zoom
([WIN-12](../../window-shell/#win-12)): Editora, Termina and Mirada zoom their content only (the text, the terminal's
font, the image), and Torrenta, Modula and nux have no zoom. So no app's menus follow one either
([MENU-16](../../menus/#menu-16)): Editora's and Mirada's, the two that set the font on `.context-menu`, write 14 px
as a fixed size. And no app records its by-hand checks ([PAUTA-18](../../conformance/#pauta-18)): the `checked` list in
`pauta.json` is new, and until an app has one, nothing tells a by-hand rule someone checked from one nobody looked
at. And no app meets all of [CLI-12](../../command-line/#cli-12): the template comes nearest, and refuses a
launcher's JVM options with every other unknown one.

## SHOULDs open in several apps

- **Windows `.msi` signing** ([REL-17](../../release/#rel-17)): no app signs its MSI yet; the certificate isn't
  chosen.
- **Settings in the macOS application menu** ([WIN-15](../../window-shell/#win-15)): only Mirada puts it there, with a
  key equivalent of its own where the rule asks for the active keymap's chord in the row's title. Editora, Termina,
  Torrenta, Modula and nux have no such row.
- **Maven's repository cached by every `setup-java` step** ([REL-25](../../release/#rel-25)): Editora, Termina and
  Torrenta each have a step without `cache: maven`.
- **The same short flags** ([CLI-13](../../command-line/#cli-13)): Termina and Torrenta take `-h`, `-v` and `-V`.
  Editora has no `-v` and Mirada no short flags; Modula and nux have no flags at all.
- **One tooltip delay** ([TBAR-14](../../toolbars-and-tooltips/#tbar-14)): no app sets 400 ms on every tooltip.
  Termina sets it on its icon buttons and leaves its palette's at JavaFX's second; Editora sets 300 to 400 ms on a
  few, Mirada 250 ms on one, and the rest set none.
- **The find command finding the next match, and every match painted** ([FIND-04](../../templates/find-bar/#find-04),
  [FIND-05](../../templates/find-bar/#find-05)): Editora's terminal find bar refocuses its field and its console
  bar has no command; Termina's refocuses too. A Debug Log drawn in a `TextArea` shows one match at a time.
- **Page Up and Page Down in the palette** ([PAL-06](../../templates/command-palette/#pal-06)): Torrenta's, Modula's
  and nux's palettes have neither.
- **The config read begun before the toolkit** ([PERF-16](../../performance/#perf-16)): Editora starts it in `main`
  and `start()` takes the result. Torrenta, Modula and nux load their settings inside `start()`; Termina reads them
  in `init()`, after the toolkit is up; Mirada's earlier read only happens in its native image.
- **A pinned toolbar tail** ([TBAR-15](../../toolbars-and-tooltips/#tbar-15)): Torrenta keeps Simple mode and Settings
  inside its `ToolBar`, so a narrow window overflows them before its icons.
- **Toolbar buttons that follow their command** ([TBAR-17](../../toolbars-and-tooltips/#tbar-17)): the buttons Editora
  adds from its catalog (Commit, Problems and the rest) stay enabled when their command can't act.
- **The chord in a stripe button's accessible name** ([TBAR-12](../../toolbars-and-tooltips/#tbar-12)): Editora binds
  it to the tool window's title alone.
- **Settings Sync** ([SYNC-01](../../templates/settings-sync/#sync-01)): Modula, Mirada and nux don't have it.
- **The Message Log as an in-scene card** ([MLOG-08](../../templates/message-log/#mlog-08)): Termina's is a separate
  window keeping 500 entries, and Torrenta's card is 640 × 300, not 773 × 336.

## How to read it

<div class="levels-legend">
  <p><span class="lvl lvl-must">MUST</span> A release blocker for a new app.</p>
  <p><span class="lvl lvl-should">SHOULD</span> Expected unless the app has a stated reason.</p>
  <p><span class="lvl lvl-may">MAY</span> A pattern worth copying when the app grows into it.</p>
</div>
